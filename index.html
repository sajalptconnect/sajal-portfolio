About Me

Who am i ??

I'm Sajal,a Web Developer passionate about building modern, high-performing websites that combine clean design with exceptional functionality. I specialize in WordPress, Elementor, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, PHP, and hosting management, creating responsive, SEO-friendly websites that help businesses grow online. With a strong focus on performance, user experience, and attention to detail, I enjoy turning ideas into impactful digital products.

"A great website is as much about the relationship as it is about the code."

From custom Elementor layouts to hand-coded functionality in PHP, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, I handle the full website lifecycle — planning, design, development, hosting setup, and deployment. I've managed server environments across Hostinger, GoDaddy, and A2 Hosting, and I know how to keep a site running smoothly long after launch.
